Your ads × your flow

Your ads promise a 3D baby, her size every week and a calendar of what to do. Every install meets name, sign-up and the same paywall.

The WeMoms ads live in the Meta Ad Library come from the page “Mom-to-be 2025: I'm Pregnant”: 25 active, all with one text, “Best Pregnancy App 2026 👑 · Track your baby in 3D · Checklists for each trimester”, and an Install now link to the App Store. The videos are what differ: most show the 3D baby, nine show moms reacting to their baby’s size this week, and three show the pregnancy calendar or a doctor. Every install still opens on the notification prompt, “What's your name?” and “What brings you here?”, whichever video she saw.

Flow Builder can open a different flow by the campaign an install came from. The three phones below skip the question the ad already answered and open on what the video promised.
